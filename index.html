HOW TO PLAY

  • Hop: press and hold on a grip to charge — release to launch.
  • Swing: in mid-air, drag and release to fire a web and swing. Drag again mid-swing to grab a new web without losing speed, or just tap to let go.
  • Punch: tap while flying near an enemy to knock it out of your way.
  • 1-UP: grab the spinning coin for an extra life.
